The Challenge

Improving confidence for high-consideration purchases

Redesigning the product detail page was a top priority for abc carpet & home leadership. Their products are high-consideration purchases with average order values over $700 — users needed more reassurance throughout the buying journey to convert. Senior leadership framed it simply:

"How might we improve shopper confidence to buy high-consideration products online?"
Research

Three findings that shaped the redesign

I proactively created a detailed research tracker outlining responsibilities. This kept the project efficient across an in-house and an external team.

1.
FullStory revealed that the most-clicked items on the existing page were at the bottom.
2.
Personas and conversion data clarified who our target audience was and where they dropped off.
3.
Competitor analysis surfaced patterns in how other high-consideration retailers built trust.
Ideation

UX Strategy

Based on the research, I defined the overall structure and UX strategy for the redesigned page. I iterated on multiple strategic approaches, presenting variations to stakeholders, evaluating pros and cons of each direction, and aligning on what best supported the brand and the buyer's journey.

  • Hero — lifestyle imagery, specs, and add-to-cart elevated to the top fold
  • Inspiration — curated sources, items that are hard to find and reduce the section format
  • Storytelling — brand values and sourcing story to build emotional connection
  • Discovery — recommendations and customer service touchpoints
Ideation

UI Explorations

Throughout this process I led the design effort: guiding and iterating with the agency's design engineers, providing feedback, and ensuring the work was optimised for build.

We agreed that an A/B test would be the most effective way to validate strategic directions, and I advocated for it with stakeholders to secure approval before moving into build.

Results

The A/B test spoke for itself

Once the designs, data, and content were fully approved, I led the handoff and partnered with the agency to bring the vision to life. The build required extensive QA and close cross-team collaboration.

After running the test for just over two weeks, the results were clear:

1.
Revenue increased by 81%.
2.
Average order value jumped 35%.
3.
Per-session value nearly doubled.

With equal experiment sessions on both sides, the results were unambiguous — the redesign based on user-centered thinking was a measurable success.

Reflection

What the numbers validated

The numbers validated the approach, but the process made them possible.

1.
Thoughtful phasing matters: moving too fast early meant scrambling at launch. Front-loading alignment saves time at the back end.
2.
A/B testing is only the beginning. Phase two is where the real work happens: refining the design and improving usability based on what the data surfaces.
3.
Designing with engineering in mind accelerates delivery — designs that align with how components are coded reduce friction from day one.
